Trade Brief

Conviction turns "I think chips are on the rise" into an agent that trades it. Before any agent gets your money, the idea has to survive four numbers: entry, stop, target, size.

The idea

Sizing is the whole tool. If the stop is 5% away, the position is small — that is the point, not a bug.
