# Nine working browser tools, rebuilt from what AI startups actually sell

**Free live preview:** https://8-321.github.io/micro-tools/

Each tool is modelled on the *real* product flow of a YC-backed AI startup — not on a screenshot. You paste your own data, you get a usable output, and you can copy or export it. Everything runs in the browser tab: **no account, no API key, no server, nothing uploaded.**

## What you get

A single downloadable bundle:

- `index.html` — hub page that links all nine tools
- 9 standalone tool pages (one folder each, plain HTML/CSS/JS)
- `assets/base.css` + `assets/base.js` — shared, readable source
- `README.md` — what each tool does and where the logic lives
- `LICENSE.md` — personal + commercial use, no resale of the source as-is

Every page is dependency-free. Open `index.html` from disk and it works offline.

## The nine tools

**01 · Inbox → Actions** — benchmark: Caddy
Paste a messy chat thread. Get tasks with due dates, questions that need a reply, and drafts you can send.

**02 · Visa Pack Builder** — benchmark: Veeza AI
Pick route and citizenship. Get the document checklist, timeline, fees and the reasons applications get refused.

**03 · Group Plan Finder** — benchmark: Anywhere / Alfi
Paste the group chat. It reads who is free when, ranks the slots, and writes the invite plus a booking brief.

**04 · Career Story Builder** — benchmark: Pluto
Ten questions, then a structured profile: pitches, proof, what stays private, and a warm-intro email.

**05 · Application Kit** — benchmark: Tsenta
Paste a job description and your resume. Get the keyword gap, rewritten bullets, a cover letter and screening answers.

**06 · Backtest Lab** — benchmark: Scalar Field
Paste daily prices, choose a rule, see the equity curve, CAGR, max drawdown, win rate versus buy-and-hold.

**07 · Portfolio X-Ray** — benchmark: Volaren
Holdings in, concentration and drift out — plus the exact rebalancing trades to get back to your targets.

**08 · Trade Brief** — benchmark: Conviction
One sentence of an idea plus entry, stop and target. Get position size, R multiple, payoff and a journal entry.

**09 · Alert Forge** — benchmark: Instinct
Turn "tell me when X breaks Y on volume" into a Pine Script v5 alert, a plain-English rule and a confluence score.

## Who it is for

- Operators who live in chat threads and need the tasks out of them
- Job seekers who want a repeatable application kit instead of rewriting from scratch
- Retail investors who want to check a rule or a portfolio without opening a terminal
- Builders who want a clean, readable starting point instead of a framework

## Honest limits

- These are **not** the products they are modelled on, and they do not use their names or logos as branding.
- No live market data, no broker connection, no automatic submission or booking. Those need APIs and accounts; the tools deliberately stop at the part you can do offline.
- Backtest numbers are close-to-close, no slippage, no fees. They are a sanity check, not a performance promise.
- Not financial, legal or immigration advice.
